## Adding Real Photographs Later
When uploading library photographs via `/admin`:
1. Use only photographs owned by the Vidyalaya or openly licensed images (e.g. Wikimedia Commons CC-BY/CC0, StoryWeaver CC-BY) and record their attribution in this file.
2. Never upload identifiable student photographs without authorised school/guardian consent.
3. Always provide descriptive **Alt Text** in the Admin upload form.
